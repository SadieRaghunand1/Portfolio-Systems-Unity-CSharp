# Portfolio-Systems

This is a repository including snippets of systems I have made for projects over that past several years.  Each individual system's files are contained in their own folder, with an in depth explanation of the system. An overview of each system's context within their projects is listed below.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Birdwars

Birdwars is an in-progress game that has been worked on by a team of one designer and one programmer, myself. The team is currently growing to include other disciplines and talent.  The premise is a multiplayer game where players are placed on an island full of birds with the mission of taking as many high quality photos of the local bird species as possible to get the best readership in their nature journal.  Players compete against the clock and each other to get good photos, find rare birds, explore environments, and sabatoge each other's photos.  Currently, player controls, in-game camera functionality, photo scoring, photo storage, and the beginings of bird movement and behavior has been implemented.

Further functionality for voice chat, complex, research-based bird AIs (unique to each species), purchasable items, and player interaction is planned.

## Mythkins

Mythkins is a mobile Android game worked on by a team of 9 artists, designers, and programmers for the course Casual Game Production.  In the game, the player temporarily takes ownership of their aunt's mythical animal santuary, home to griffins, krakens, and cerberuses.  They must play minigames with each animals to increase each santuary inhabitant's affection score to fully rehabilitate each to be released.  They also must pay attention to their environment, keeping an eye for broken facilities, each repaired with a minigame, to avoid the santuary from falling apart.
